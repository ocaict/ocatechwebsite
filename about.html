About OCATECH Digital Solutions

Bridging the digital divide in Nigeria through accessible, hands-on, high-standard technology education and reliable engineering services.

Who We Are

Empowering Builders, Problem Solvers & Digital Leaders

OCATECH Digital Solutions is a premier ICT training hub and technology services enterprise based at 62 New Market Road, Onitsha North, Anambra State. We were established to provide practical, employment-focused digital education that directly translates into real-world capability.

We believe technology education should not be trapped in abstract theory. From computer literacy to advanced artificial intelligence engineering, every single student at OCATECH is guided through building tangible projects, writing working code, configuring live hardware, and mastering industry-standard workflows.
